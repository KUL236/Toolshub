📚 TOOLHUB 5D GUIDE

Documentation

Everything you need to use ToolHub 5D confidently, from choosing a tool to understanding how your data is handled.

Getting startedTool categoriesData & processingAccount & settingsHelp & support
01

Getting started

Search from the top bar or open a category from the sidebar. Select a tool, follow its on-screen instructions, then download or copy the result. Most tools work without an account.

🔍 Search🛠️ Choose a tool⚡ Use instantly
02

Tool categories

ToolHub groups utilities so you can find the right workflow quickly:

  • AI & Smart: writing, summarizing and productivity helpers.
  • PDF & Image: browser-based conversion, editing and compression.
  • Developer: formatting, encoding and coding utilities.
  • Finance, Health & Student: calculators and everyday planning tools.
  • Games & Fun: local games and lightweight entertainment.
03

Data & processing

Whenever possible, files are processed inside your browser. The page shows when a tool needs an internet connection or an external service. Do not upload confidential files to any online service unless you are comfortable with its terms.

🔒 Tip: clear your recent list and browser downloads when using a shared device.
04

Account, themes & preferences

Sign in only when you want account features such as profile access or cloud-backed preferences. Your theme, favorites and recent tools can be remembered locally on this device. Open the palette icon to change themes, colors and light/dark mode.

05

Help & support

If a tool does not behave as expected, refresh the page, check the internet toggle and try a smaller input. Report bugs or request features through .

🛡️ PRIVACY FIRST

Privacy & Policy

A clear summary of what ToolHub 5D collects, what stays on your device and what choices you have.

🧭

Our approach

ToolHub 5D is designed to be useful without requiring an account. We aim to collect the minimum information needed to operate, secure and improve the service.

💻

Local processing

Many calculators, converters, games and editors run in your browser. Files selected for local tools are not uploaded by the app. Results may remain temporarily in page memory until you close or refresh the page.

👤

Accounts

If you sign in, authentication and profile data are handled by our configured authentication provider. We use it to provide account features and do not sell personal information.

🍪

Storage & cookies

Preferences such as theme, favorites and recent tools may be saved in localStorage. Browser settings can clear this data. Essential session or security storage may be used when account features are enabled.

🔗

Third-party services

Some optional tools load libraries, AI providers, fonts or authentication services from third parties. Their own privacy policies apply when a request leaves your browser.

📩

Your choices

You can avoid signing in, clear local preferences, disable optional internet tools and contact us about privacy questions through the feedback page.

Policy updates

We may update this page when features or laws change. The latest version will always be available here.
